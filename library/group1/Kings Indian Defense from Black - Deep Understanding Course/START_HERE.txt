KING'S INDIAN DEFENSE FROM BLACK - DEEP UNDERSTANDING COURSE
==================================================================

18 PDF volumes | 108 deep lessons | 54 legal reference lines | 540 PDF pages

This is a separately written, concept-first Black repertoire. It does not revise or
duplicate the earlier compact King's Indian book. Every scenario is taught twice:
first as a recognition problem, then as an execution problem. Each lesson contains
two diagram pages, a move-by-move walkthrough, exercises, and an answer key.

STARTING REPERTOIRE
-------------------
Against the Classical system, the course anchors Black with ...e5 and the main
...Nc6 / ...Ne7 closed-center structures. It also explains the ...Nbd7 alternative.
Against Saemisch, Four Pawns, Fianchetto, Averbakh, Makogonov, London, Torre,
English, and Reti move orders, use the dedicated volumes rather than one setup blindly.
Volume 18 explains the Pirc bridge against 1.e4 and the important structural differences.

HOW TO STUDY
------------
1. Read one four-page lesson and reconstruct the board from memory.
2. Import KID_BLACK_REPERTOIRE_FOR_FRITZ.pgn into Fritz for replay and sparring.
3. Answer the workbook page before viewing its answer key.
4. Review after 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days.
5. Verify sharp forcing branches with Fritz or a current database before tournament use.
6. Analyze every serious game and add only the positions you actually need.
