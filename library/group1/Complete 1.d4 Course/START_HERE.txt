COMPLETE 1.d4 COURSE
====================

15 PDF volumes - 626 total pages.
92 unique training positions and 12 constructed model games.

RECOMMENDED ORDER
-----------------
1. Begin with Volume 1 and learn the repertoire map.
2. Study Volumes 2-6 for 1...d5 and 1...Nf6 2...e6 systems.
3. Study Volumes 7-11 for Indian defenses, the Dutch, and rare replies.
4. Use Volumes 12-14 to connect structures, tactics, and complete games.
5. Finish with Volume 15 and maintain the repertoire from your own games.

TRAINING METHOD
---------------
Say the move and its purpose before revealing the answer.
Review misses after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, and 60 days.
Analyze your own game before turning on an engine.
Add new opponent moves to a personal copy of the repertoire PGN.
