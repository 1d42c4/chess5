COMPLETE HIPPOPOTAMUS SYSTEM COURSE
=====================================

15 PDF volumes - 626 total pages.
99 taught repertoire branches, 91 unique training positions, and 12 constructed model games.

RECOMMENDED ORDER
-----------------
1. Begin with Volume 1. Learn the setup as a decision tree, not an automatic sequence.
2. Study Volumes 2-5 for opponent systems, transpositions, and move-order emergencies.
3. Study Volumes 6-10 by break: e5, c5, f5, b5, maneuvers, and anti-Hippo attacks.
4. Use Volume 11 to see the system from White's side and understand how it is attacked.
5. Use Volumes 12-15 for structures, tactics, model games, mixed tests, and maintenance.

TRAINING METHOD
---------------
Say the move, its purpose, and the opponent's best reply before revealing the answer.
For Black positions compare e5, c5, f5, and b5 before selecting a quiet move.
Review misses after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, and 60 days.
Analyze your own game before turning on an engine.
Add new opponent moves to a personal copy of the repertoire PGN.
