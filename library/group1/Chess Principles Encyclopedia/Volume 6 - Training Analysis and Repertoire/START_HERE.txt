CHESS PRINCIPLES ENCYCLOPEDIA - VOLUME 6
============================================

25 individual PDFs | 6 pages each | 150 pages total

HOW TO STUDY THIS VOLUME
------------------------
1. Study one PDF at a time and restate the rule without looking.
2. Explain the causal mechanism: why should the rule improve the position?
3. Set up the board and generate three candidates before reading your notes.
4. State the limitation and calculate the opponent's strongest forcing reply.
5. Find the pattern in a slow game, then review after 3, 7, 14, and 30 days.

IMPORTANT
---------
Principles organize attention; they do not override tactics. Use this decision order:
legal and tactical survival, king safety, forcing initiative, material, activity,
structure, space, and finally the general rule as a tiebreaker.
