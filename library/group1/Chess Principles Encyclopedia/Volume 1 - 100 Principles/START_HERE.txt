==========================================

100 individual principles | 6 pages each | 600 pages total

WHY STRONG PLAYERS CAN SEEM MILES AHEAD
-----------------------------------------
They do not merely know more slogans. They notice meaningful patterns sooner, generate better
candidate moves, calculate the opponent's best resistance, evaluate resulting positions more
accurately, and make fewer ordinary errors. Their knowledge is compressed into connected chunks:
structure + piece placement + plans + tactics + exceptions.

HOW TO USE THIS LIBRARY
-----------------------
1. Study one principle, not ten, in a session.
2. Answer the self-test without looking back.
3. Find the principle in one of your own games.
4. Review after 3, 7, 14, and 30 days.
5. Treat every principle as a default that must survive tactical verification.

Principles are most useful in this order: tactical survival, king safety, forcing initiative,
material, activity, structure, space, and finally the general slogan as a tiebreaker.
