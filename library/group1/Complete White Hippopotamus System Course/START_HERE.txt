TRAINING METHOD
---------------
Say the move, its purpose, and Black's best reply before revealing the answer.
Compare e4, d4, c4, and f4 before choosing another quiet setup move.
Review misses after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, and 60 days.
Analyze your own game before turning on an engine.
Add new Black responses to a personal copy of the repertoire PGN.
