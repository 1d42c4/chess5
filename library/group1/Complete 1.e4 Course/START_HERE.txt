COMPLETE 1.e4 COURSE
====================

15 PDF volumes - 626 total pages.
97 unique training positions and 12 constructed model games.

RECOMMENDED ORDER
-----------------
1. Begin with Volume 1 and learn the response map.
2. Study Volumes 2-3 for 1...e5 and the open games.
3. Study Volumes 4-6 for the Alapin and major Open Sicilian structures.
4. Study Volumes 7-11 for the French, Caro-Kann, Pirc, Modern, Scandinavian, Alekhine, and rare replies.
5. Use Volumes 12-15 for structures, tactics, model games, mixed tests, and maintenance.

TRAINING METHOD
---------------
Say the move and its purpose before revealing the answer.
Review misses after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, and 60 days.
Analyze your own game before turning on an engine.
Add new opponent moves to a personal copy of the repertoire PGN.
