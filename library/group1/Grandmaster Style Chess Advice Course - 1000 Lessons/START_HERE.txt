GRANDMASTER-STYLE CHESS ADVICE COURSE - 1,000 LESSONS
=========================================================

20 PDF volumes | 200 five-part modules | 1,000 advice lessons | 1,320 PDF pages

This is an original teaching synthesis, not a collection of attributed quotations.
Every module teaches one idea in five passes: core advice, recognition, board use,
exceptions, and training. Every lesson includes a logical reason, use conditions,
a failure mode, a board question, and a practical drill.

RECOMMENDED STUDY
-----------------
1. Study one five-part module at a time, not isolated slogans.
2. Use a real board and answer the board question before reading the page again.
3. Review after 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days.
4. Apply the module in slow games and record where it appeared.
5. Treat tactics and exact calculation as the final authority.

PACING OPTIONS
--------------
Deep course: one module per week (200 weeks).
Steady course: two modules per week (100 weeks).
Intensive course: one volume every four weeks (80 weeks).
Daily advice: one lesson per day, with the other four module pages during review.
