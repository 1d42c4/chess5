CHESS MASTERY COMPLETE COURSE LIBRARY
=====================================

15 integrated PDF courses designed for phone, tablet, and computer study.

RECOMMENDED ORDER
-----------------
1. Anti-Blunder, Tactics, and Calculation
2. Essential Endgames and Pawn Structures
3. Planning, Attacking, Defense, Conversion, and Exchanges
4. Piece Mastery, Model Games, and Opening Transitions
5. Practical Psychology and the Workbook

STUDY RULE
----------
Do not rush through the library. Study one unit, retrieve it without looking,
apply it in a slow game, analyze the decision, and review after 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days.
