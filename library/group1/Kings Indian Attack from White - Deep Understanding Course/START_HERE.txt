KING'S INDIAN ATTACK FROM WHITE - DEEP UNDERSTANDING COURSE
===========================================================

18 PDF volumes | 108 deep lessons | 54 legal annotated study lines | 540 PDF pages

This is a concept-first White system course. It teaches the shared KIA shell, the classical e5 attack, and the structural adaptations required against French, Sicilian, ...g6, ...e5, ...b6, Caro-Kann, and Philidor-style setups.
Every scenario is taught twice: first as a recognition problem, then as an execution problem.
Each lesson contains two diagram pages, a move-by-move walkthrough, exercises, and an answer key.

STARTING REPERTOIRE
-------------------
Use 1.Nf3 for maximum flexibility or 1.e4 when you specifically want French and Sicilian KIA structures. Build g3, Bg2, O-O, d3, Nbd2, e4, and Re1 only when Black’s center allows it. Classify Black’s freeing break before selecting e5, c3-d4, c4, f4, or a kingside pawn storm.

HOW TO STUDY
------------
1. Read one four-page lesson and reconstruct the board from memory.
2. Import KINGS_INDIAN_ATTACK_WHITE_FOR_FRITZ.pgn into Fritz for replay and sparring.
3. Answer the workbook page before viewing its answer key.
4. Review after 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days.
5. Verify sharp forcing branches with Fritz or a current database before tournament use.
6. Analyze every serious game and repair the first position you could not explain.
