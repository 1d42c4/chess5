COMPLETE CHESS OPENING ATLAS - A00 TO E99
===========================================

This library contains 500 individual PDFs and 3,810 named opening lines.

HOW TO USE IT
-------------
1. Pick one opening you actually meet in your games.
2. Study pages 1-2 first: description, goals, pawn breaks, and danger signs.
3. Rebuild the featured diagram on a board without looking.
4. Browse the named-line catalogue only after you understand the position.
5. Import ALL_3810_NAMED_OPENING_LINES.pgn into Fritz or another chess program.
6. Play training games and explain every move in words before checking an engine.

FOLDER MAP
----------
A - flank openings, English, Benoni systems, and Dutch
B - semi-open games, Caro-Kann, and Sicilian
C - French and open games after 1.e4 e5
D - closed games, Queen's Gambit, Slav, and Grunfeld
E - Catalan, Queen's Indian, Nimzo-Indian, and King's Indian

A practical recommendation: do not try to memorize all 500. Use the atlas as a map.
Build a small personal repertoire, then consult the relevant code when a new opening
appears in your games.

FILES
-----
OPENING_ATLAS_MANIFEST.csv - one row per PDF, including page count and path
ALL_3810_NAMED_OPENING_LINES.csv - searchable source catalogue with FEN positions
ALL_3810_NAMED_OPENING_LINES.pgn - importable Fritz training/reference file
SOURCE_AND_SCOPE.txt - dataset, license, and limitations
OPENING_ATLAS_INDEX.html - clickable offline index
